Private Meal Rotation diagnostics

This standalone tool reads a backup on your device. It does not upload anything, contact the app, or change your recipes, site data, or rotation history. You can disconnect from the internet before using it.

  1. In the app, use Settings and backups → Export backup. Keep that backup private; do not send the original file.
  2. Choose the backup below. Select the affected group and the three recipes by their names. Names appear only in these local selectors.
  3. Generate and review the anonymized report, then download or copy that report to share with ChatGPT.

The report omits names, ingredients, instructions, URLs, notes, photos, file names, and actual cooking dates. IDs, group names, labels, and plan names become aliases. It includes queue order, relative cooking-date ranks, eligibility/link metadata, slot choices, Favorites credits, and up to three saved rotation snapshots. Only share the generated report after reviewing it.
